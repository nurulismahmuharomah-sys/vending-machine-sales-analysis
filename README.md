# Vending Machine Sales Analysis Dashboard

**Program:** RevoU Tech Academy – Kampus Merdeka  
**Batch:** February 2024  
**Project Type:** Collaborative Group Project  
**Focus:** Data Analytics & Dashboard Development

## Project Overview

This project was developed collaboratively as part of the RevoU Tech Academy program under the Kampus Merdeka initiative, Batch February 2024. It focuses on analyzing vending machine sales performance through an interactive dashboard to generate business insights and support data-driven decision-making.

The project covers business background analysis, hypothesis development, sales performance monitoring, and interactive data exploration to evaluate product performance, regional contributions, and monthly sales trends.

## Key Features

- **Business Background & Hypotheses:** Establishes the business context, analytical questions, and hypotheses guiding the analysis.
- **Sales Performance Overview:** Summarizes total sales, total transactions, and total products.
- **Top 10 Product Analysis:** Identifies the highest- and lowest-performing products based on total sales.
- **Product Sales Exploration:** Enables users to search and explore product-level sales data.
- **Sales by Category:** Compares sales distribution across product categories.
- **Sales by Region:** Evaluates sales contributions and performance across vending machine locations.
- **Sales by Machine:** Examines sales performance by individual vending machine.
- **Monthly Sales Trends:** Tracks monthly sales patterns by category and region using adjustable month filters.

## Analytical Objectives

1. Evaluate overall sales performance and transaction activity.
2. Identify top- and low-performing products.
3. Compare sales across product categories, regions, and vending machines.
4. Explore monthly sales trends and variations.
5. Develop actionable business insights and recommendations based on the findings.
